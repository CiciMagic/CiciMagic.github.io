*I write to think.*

*Curiosity often begins with a question: How does it work? Does it really make sense in practice? How might others see it differently? I explore these questions through reading, conversations, interviews, observation, and sometimes simply following a thread wherever it leads.*

*Writing helps me make sense of what I find. It forces me to connect ideas, test assumptions, and turn scattered observations into something clearer.*

*This blog is a record of that process—not a collection of definitive answers, but a space for questions, discoveries, and ideas still taking shape.*
